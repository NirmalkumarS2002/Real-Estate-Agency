circle Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what most people ask us before their first visit. Still have a question? Reach out through the form above and we'll get back to you directly.

How soon will an agent contact me? expand_more

Most enquiries submitted through the form or a call are responded to within 24 hours on business days. Urgent enquiries can call our front desk directly for a same-day response.

Do you charge a fee for property viewings? expand_more

No, property viewings are free for buyers and tenants. Our fees are structured around successful transactions and are explained upfront before any agreement is signed.

Can you help me sell a property in a different city branch's area? expand_more

Yes, all three of our branches share listings and coordinate on cross-area transactions, so your agent can help regardless of which office they're based at.

What documents should I bring for a first consultation? expand_more

For buyers, a valid ID and proof of budget approval helps. For sellers, property title documents and past tax receipts speed up the initial valuation.
